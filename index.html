Filament Spool Re-wind Machine

Advanced Design Engineering Project 202 - Semester 2

Project Objectives

This project involves the continued development of a 3D printer filament unwind/wind machine, transitioning the design from initial prototype to a manufacturing-ready retail product. The primary focus is on mechanical reliability, regulatory compliance, and enhanced user experience.

  • Automation: Automated determination of spool status (full/empty).
  • Inclusivity: Implementation of a vertically adjustable mechanism to suit individual user height requirements.
  • Storage: Integration of a portable workbench capable of segregating four full and four empty spools.

Engineering Methodology

Compliance & Standards

Detailed review of relevant regulatory standards and directives to ensure commercial viability and safety.

DFMA & Reliability

Application of Design for Manufacture, Assembly, Reliability, and Maintenance (DFM/DFA) principles.

Sustainability

Strategic selection of components and materials to maximize environmental impact during the product lifecycle.

Technical Development

Primary Drive System

The system utilizes a single Oriental Motor 25W AC induction motor with a 120:1 reduction gearhead for high-torque delivery.

Control & Feedback

Implementation of low-voltage electrical devices for automated process monitoring and status signaling.

Virtual Testing & Simulation

Virtual testing is employed to ensure mechanism effectiveness prior to physical assembly. Our analysis includes kinematic validation and CAD integration.
