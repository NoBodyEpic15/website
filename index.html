Filament-Spool Re-wind Machine

Design Engineering Project 202 - ATU Sligo

An automated, inclusive, and sustainable solution for 3D printing material management.

Discover Project

Project Objectives

This project focused on the development of a Filament-Spool Re-wind Machine, transitioning the design into a highly functional, safe, and sustainable product.

Automation & Safety

Integrated sensors and a mechanical limit switch to ensure the spool is properly seated before the motor can operate.

Inclusivity (DEI)

Tool-less spool changing system and clear color-coded controls to accommodate users of all experience levels.

Ergonomic Table

Adjustable height workbench with integrated storage for 8 spools (4 full, 4 empty).

Engineering Methodology

Sustainability (Goal 12)

We optimized our 3D printed parts to reduce filament use and utilized laser-cutting for flat components (gears/connecting rods) to reduce material waste and energy consumption. We selected recyclable thermoplastics like PLA and PETG.

DFMA & Manufacturability

The design uses bolts instead of adhesives, allowing for easy disassembly, maintenance, and part replacement. Bearings and springs can be replaced individually without discarding the whole system.

Safety Integration

A physical safety guard shield was designed to cover the top gears. An infrared sensor monitors the filament path, and automation prevents snapping or overloading without human supervision.

Technical Development & Testing

Control & Circuitry

We developed a custom circuit featuring an Arduino Uno, an ultrasonic sensor for real-time distance monitoring, and a MOSFET/diode setup. This ensures safe motor modulation and protects the electronics from inductive back-EMF (voltage spikes).

  • ● Precision: Potentiometer for variable speed control.
  • ● Safety: Flyback diode integrated for motor protection.
  • ● Feedback: LED status indicators for system state.

Virtual Testing & FEA Simulation

Gear & Rail Assembly — FEA

To verify the structural integrity of the reciprocating filament guide assembly, a static structural analysis was carried out in ANSYS on the gear and rail components. The mesh comprised 95,309 nodes and 19,845 elements.

Under the applied loading conditions, the maximum total deformation recorded was just \( 7.614 \times 10^{-3} \) mm — negligible relative to the component dimensions. Peak shear stress reached 0.342 MPa, well within the elastic limits. The safety factor was reported at the ANSYS solver cap of 15, confirming a substantial margin against failure.

Worm Gear — FEA

A separate static structural simulation was run on the worm gear assembly using a mesh of 30,278 nodes and 16,434 elements. The peak von Mises stress recorded was just 0.033 MPa — orders of magnitude below the yield strength of any standard engineering material.

Maximum deformation was \( 3.035 \times 10^{-4} \) mm (approx. 0.304 µm). The safety factor was capped at 15 across all nodes, meaning the structure is significantly over-designed relative to the load case analysed.

Cantilever Shaft — FEA & Hand Calc

The shaft (50 mm diameter) was loaded with a conservative 700 N downward tip load — representative of a person leaning on the assembly. Hand calculations \( \sigma = \frac{M \cdot c}{I} \) predicted a bending stress of 6.50 MPa.

ANSYS returned 7.15 MPa for PLA, showing a satisfactory 10% agreement with hand calculations. While PLA has a safety factor of 3.79, Structural Steel was chosen as the final material to minimize tip deflection (66x less than PLA) and ensure an "indestructible" safety factor of 38.5.

Prototyping & Media

Physical Mechanism

Combining 3D printed components with laser-cut plates (PMMA/Acrylic) significantly improved production time and accuracy while reducing material waste.

Tension Control

A dedicated filament tension control mechanism utilizes Igus bearings (BB-604-B180-30-ES) and custom 3D printed springs for optimal performance.

ATU Makerspace

Iterative testing and fabrication were heavily supported by the ATU Makerspace equipment, allowing us to test and fix gear alignment issues swiftly.
